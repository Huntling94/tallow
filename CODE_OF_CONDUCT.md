# Code of Conduct

Tallow adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). This page summarises it in plain language; the Covenant is the full text that applies.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and respectful, including when you disagree.
- Give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Own your mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people around it.

## What is not acceptable

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel run by the project) and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to GitHub: open the **...** menu on the comment, issue or pull request, or go to the person's profile, and choose **Report abuse**. GitHub's safety team reviews every report, and your identity is not shared with the person you report.

The maintainer also moderates the project directly, by hiding or removing content, locking conversations and blocking accounts that break this code.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. The enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant apply.
